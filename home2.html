Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, Answered with Care

Everything you need to know about receiving convenient, personalized healthcare at home.

What services does Pulse Haven provide?

Pulse Haven provides convenient in-home health monitoring, including blood pressure checks, vital sign measurements, medication reviews and wellness visits.

How can I schedule an in-home vitals check?

You can schedule a visit through our website by choosing a convenient appointment time. You can also manage your appointments through your patient dashboard.

What should I prepare before a nurse visits my home?

Find a comfortable, quiet place to sit and keep any relevant medication information or previous health records handy. Our nurse will guide you through the measurements.

Can I track my blood pressure and vital signs online?

Yes. Your patient dashboard lets you review your recorded blood pressure and other measured vital signs, along with your visit notes and appointment details.

Can I reschedule or manage my appointments?

Yes. You can manage your scheduled visits and appointment reminders through the patient dashboard, subject to the available scheduling options.
