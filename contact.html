COMMON QUESTIONS

Care, clarity, and confidence at every step.

Find helpful answers about home visits, health tracking, available services, and what to expect from Pulse Haven.

01 What can I expect during a Pulse Haven home visit?

Your visit is designed around your needs, with a convenient at-home check of your vitals and supportive guidance from our care team.

02 How does Pulse Haven help me keep track of my health over time?

Your visit information and vitals history can be organized through your patient dashboard, making it easier to follow changes and stay connected with your care.

03 Can I choose the type of care or visit I need?

Yes. You can enquire about vitals checks, medication reviews, and wellness visits based on the type of support you are looking for.

04 What happens after I request a Pulse Haven visit?

Our team can review your enquiry, help identify the appropriate service, and guide you through the next steps for arranging your visit.
